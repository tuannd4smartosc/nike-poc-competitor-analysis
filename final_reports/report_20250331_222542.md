# Nike
## Introduction:
Nike, Inc. is a global sportswear giant that traces its origins back to 1962 when former University of Oregon track-and-field athlete Phil Knight founded the company. The company officially became Nike, Inc. on May 30, 1971. Based in Beaverton, Oregon, NIKE, Inc. includes the Nike, Converse, and Jordan brands. Nike is one of the leading sports brands in the world, known for its innovative products and strong brand image.

## Asics Promotion Campaigns 2024-2025

   - Impact on Nike: Asics' association with influencers and emphasis on mental benefits could sway consumer perception away from Nike.

By examining these promotion campaigns and their potential impacts, it is evident that both Puma and Asics are actively refining their strategies to enhance their market presence and competitiveness against Nike.

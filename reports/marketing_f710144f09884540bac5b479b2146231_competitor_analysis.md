# Nike's Competitors Promotion Campaigns Analysis

## Asics Campaigns:
1. **Sale with Up to 30% Off Running Shoes**  
   - *Description:* Asics offered a sale with up to 30% off running shoes, targeting spring season customers.
   - *Impact on Nike:* This promotion could attract potential customers who might have considered Nike products, potentially leading to a decrease in Nike's market share.

2. **80s Inspired Propaganda Campaign**  
   - *Description:* Asics collaborated with Sneaker Politics for a campaign inspired by the 80s, creating a nostalgic appeal.
   - *Impact on Nike:* This unique campaign may sway customers who seek retro styles, affecting Nike's market presence in this segment.

3. **Promotional Terms with Discount Codes**  
   - *Description:* Asics offered a $60 discount on selected full-priced items with a minimum spend using a specific code.
   - *Impact on Nike:* Competing discounts may entice price-sensitive customers away from Nike to Asics.

## Adidas Campaigns:
1. **'You Got This' Campaign Addressing Sideline Pressures**  
   - *Description:* Adidas continued its 'You Got This' brand campaign to address high-pressure environments in sports.
   - *Impact on Nike:* This campaign's empowering message may resonate with athletes, potentially drawing attention away from Nike's branding.

2. **Individuality-Focused Brand Campaign**  
   - *Description:* Adidas' campaign celebrated heritage and self-expression, inviting consumers to embrace individuality.
   - *Impact on Nike:* This emphasis on self-expression could attract customers seeking unique sportswear, posing a competitive challenge for Nike.

3. **Global Brand Campaign Promoting Positive Influence**  
   - *Description:* Adidas launched a global brand campaign encouraging positive influence on and off the sports field.
   - *Impact on Nike:* This campaign highlighting positivity and community could appeal to socially conscious consumers, impacting Nike's brand positioning.

These campaigns showcase the diverse strategies employed by Asics and Adidas, posing both direct and indirect challenges to Nike's market dominance. Nike should carefully monitor competitor campaigns to adapt its marketing efforts effectively and maintain its competitive edge in the sportswear industry.
